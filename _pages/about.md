I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Institute for Advanced Study and Toulouse School of Economics ([IAST](https://www.iast.fr/)/[TSE](https://www.tse-fr.eu/fr)). 
I obtained my PhD in Economics at [UCLouvain](https://www.uclouvain.be/fr) in September 2025. 

My research investigates the historical foundations of knowledge: how ideas emerge, circulate, and combine into innovations, and how people and institutions shape these processes over time. I combine applied microeconomics with historical data to study questions at the intersection of education, migration, innovation, and long-run development.
